Instant caller ID for your VoIP line

Know who's calling before you answer

CallerFlash watches your SIP line and pops up a desktop toast for every incoming call — with the number already copied to your clipboard. One paste pulls up the caller in your CRM, browser, or ticketing system before you even say hello.

The incoming-call popup — fully customizable, draggable, and it remembers where you put it.
📞 Instant popups
A toast appears the moment your line rings — number and caller name included.
📋 Paste-ready
The caller's number lands on your clipboard automatically. Ctrl+V and go.
🔒 Private & secure
Credentials encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Signed, verified auto-updates.

Up and running in five minutes

1
 Download the installer for Windows from the releases page below.
2
 Enter your SIP credentials in Settings — presets for VoIP.ms make this a two-field job.
3
 Click Connect. When the status turns green, you're live — every call flashes on screen.
